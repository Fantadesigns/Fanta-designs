About the Project

JMA & Co Spaces had a concept that was genuinely unique — Africandi, a design philosophy that fuses the warmth and richness of African aesthetics with the clean restraint of Scandinavian minimalism. The challenge was translating that dual identity into a website that felt cohesive, not conflicted. Every layout decision, color choice and typographic detail had to honor both worlds equally.

Outcomes

The website captured the Africandi vision so well it started bringing in new clients on its own. The client loved the result and the site gave the business a professional online presence worthy of the high-end interior design market.

"Working with Chisom was one of the best decisions I made for this project. She dedicated an entire week to sitting with us, understanding the vision and designing everything together in real time. Her humility, patience and commitment to getting it right was something I've rarely seen. Honestly, I wish I had 10 of her on my team."

— Jennifer, Creative Director, JMA & Co. Spaces, Houston USA
