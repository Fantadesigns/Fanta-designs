From Zero to 3,000+ Users — Designing an eCommerce Platform From the Ground Up

Designed the complete brand identity and 400+ screens for Intasida, an eCommerce startup building smarter ways for businesses to sell, track orders and manage logistics across Nigeria.

Brand & product designer
4 months
Web & mobile

About the Project

Intasida came with nothing: no brand, no screens, no design system. The challenge was to build everything from scratch while keeping the product coherent, scalable and ready for a real market launch. As the sole designer on a team of 15, every visual decision, from the logo to the first screen, was mine to make and defend.

Outcomes

Intasida launched successfully and gained over 3,000 user signups within the first 2 months. The brand identity and product design gave the company a credible, professional presence that resonated with early users and set a strong foundation for future growth.

Process

User research

Interviewed target users to understand their logistics and selling pain points, shaping the core product experience.

High-fidelity design

Designed 400+ screens in Figma covering the full product — dashboard, orders, logistics, mobile app, and onboarding.

Brand identity & design system

Built complete brand from scratch — logo, colour system, typography, and a scalable component library.

Prototyping & Testing

Ran usability sessions to validate key flows — order creation, tracking, and dispute management — before handoff.
