Mobile app Ed-Tech Launching soon

Putting +60 years of University knowledge into every students' pockets

Designed the end-to-end mobile library app for the University of Nigeria, Nsukka — digitizing 90M+ pages of academic material for 35,000–60,000 students, lecturers, and administrative staff.

Lead product designer
2 weeks
iOS & Android

About the Project

The University of Nigeria, Nsukka had no digital presence for its library. Students had to visit in person to access materials — many of which dated back to the 1960s and were deteriorating. Over 90 million pages were repaired, scanned, and digitized. The challenge: design a mobile experience modern enough for today's students, yet respectful of decades of academic history.

Outcomes

The app is in its final stages ahead of a university-wide mandatory rollout. With 60,000+ students, lecturers, and administrative staff required to sign up, the project is targeting 50% active adoption within the first two months — making it one of the largest ed-tech launches from a Nigerian university.

Process

User research

Interviewed students, lecturers, and library staff to map real access patterns — discovering offline access and role-based views as the two most critical design requirements

High-fidelity design

Designed the full app in Figma — covering onboarding, home, search, reading experience, bookmarks, and a separate lecturer view with book sales analytics and book submissions

Design system

Built a full component library with colour tokens, typography, icons, and reusable UI patterns to ensure visual consistency across hundreds of screens

Prototyping & Testing

Ran usability sessions with students to validate navigation patterns, reading experience, and onboarding flow — iterating on feedback before final handoff to engineering
